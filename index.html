Xuejing Liu

Researcher, Qwen Team, Tongyi Lab, Alibaba Group

I work on multimodal large language models. My work sits in three places: how pretraining data is built, cleaned and mixed; how vision is wired into the language backbone; and how to evaluate models honestly. I have contributed to the Qwen series of models since Qwen2-VL.
